# Cloud on strings: four face treatments (round seven)

Artem picked the visor cloud on strings (round six, no. 1). His note: the face looked too smeared, too big and clumsy. Sheet: [`cloud-faces.png`](cloud-faces.png). Each treatment is shown large, at 64 and 32 px, and as a store list card. Single files: `cloud-faces/`. Source: `scripts/brand/cloudfaces.py`, `cloudfaces_sheet.py`. Icon only; the full kit waits for Artem's approval.

## What changed in the base

- **Cloud:** now fully inside the tile with a margin, no longer cropped at the top.
- **Island:** larger (36 px wide, previously 32). The small tree and the hanging roots were removed: at 64 px the tree read as a thorn and the roots touched the bottom edge.
- **Strings:** two pixels each, a lit line beside a shadow line, ending in a bright anchor, so they hold at 32 px.
- **Face:** kept small and crisp, secondary to the cloud, strings and island.

## Result

- **Face comparison.** Three fresh art-director judges saw the four faces and the round-six face side by side. They picked **1 (small visor)** twice and **2 (two eyes)** once. They called the slit "so faint it nearly disappears" and the shading version "smeared, reads as a shadow". All three called the round-six face "too big … looks like a TV".
- **Icon alone:** with the face this small, the icon at a glance now reads as "sky islands / skyblock" for all four. The AI part is no longer carried by the icon alone. This is the trade-off for the restraint Artem asked for.
- **List card:** every judge understood the mod and said they would probably click.
- **Harsh look check:** decent for all four, and judged not AI-made. The repeated notes: the island's isometric faces feel slightly lopsided, the cloud is a plain slab, and the background disc is faint.

Recommendation: **1, small visor**, with 2, two eyes, as the alternative. Both are crisp and stay secondary to the silhouette.

### 1. Small visor

Small visor: a short, crisp dark visor with two small eyes.

Glance (icon alone, 32 and 64 px):

> Looks like a floating grass island with a cloud on top, so I'd guess a sky-islands or skyblock-style world generation mod, or maybe a weather or cloud mod. I'd probably click it. The 64px version is clear and appealing, but at 32px it turns into a blurry blue blob.

List card:

> Almighty is a new server mod where an AI agent acts as your game master and runs the Minecraft server within the permissions you set. I'd probably click it out of curiosity, since it's the only one in the list that sounds new and unusual, and the "within the permissions you give it" line makes it feel safe to try. Harsh look check:

> The icon is clean and readable, with a consistent dark outline, a limited palette and a clear silhouette. Its weaknesses are the generic cloud with a visor, the stiff, symmetrical isometric block, the near-empty background circle, and flat grass and dirt shading with little texture or dithering. It is competent but plain and safe, with nothing distinctive to make it memorable. **Decent**

Harsh look check:

> This is cleaner than most amateur work: the outline is consistent, the palette is limited, and the cloud and island silhouettes read clearly. But the island's isometric geometry is off, because the top face and the side faces don't quite agree on perspective. The shading is flat and timid, with minimal dithering or highlights, and the soil and stone layers are generic. The cloud is a bland slab, the three beams are stiff, and the background is a muddy blue with a barely visible circle, so the whole thing feels competent but generic. Nothing here looks AI-generated, since the pixels are clean and deliberate, but it lacks the polish and personality of professional mod art. Decent.
