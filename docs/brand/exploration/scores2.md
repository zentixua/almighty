Sheet: [`scores2.png`](scores2.png). Marks are ranked by average score, each shown as icon + "Almighty". Single icons: `scores2/`. Source: `scripts/brand/round9.py`, `scores_sheet.py`.

## Method

Same as round eight ([`scores.md`](scores.md)), with no changes:
- **Pool:** 8 unlabeled marks at 64 px, each shown only as icon + "Almighty". The 5 combos join the round-eight top 3 as controls: the halo cursor, the cloud with the visor, and the halo bot.
- **Judges:** 7 fresh `claude -p` sessions, each in an empty directory, each with its own random order. They used the same prompt and scored every mark from 1 to 10 on clarity (name + icon say what the mod does), click, and look, plus one line on what the mod does.
- **Score:** the average of the three scores.

## Combos

Each combo joins the strongest cues: the halo for almighty, the agent's small visor for AI, and acting on a grass block or island for Minecraft power. The cloud is narrower in the two cloud combos, as Artem asked.

## What it means

- **The cloud family wins.** The haloed narrow cloud (combo) and the original cloud with the visor (control) tie at 6.24, with the same clarity (5.14), click (6.86) and look (6.71). The narrow cloud without a halo follows at 5.90. Adding the halo helped the narrow cloud (+0.34) and moved the reads toward "divine/god-like … world control". Narrowing alone cost a little.
- **Scores depend on the pool.** The round-eight winner, the halo cursor, scores 5.48 here (6.81 in round eight). It was judged next to stronger cloud marks and the cursor-like combo, so compare marks within one pool only.
- **Meaning against score.** The combos with the robot head (the halo bot with a beam or looking over the world) are the only ones read as "AI … that controls blocks / god-like control over the world". That is the closest to the full idea by name + icon. They score lower on look and click (5.14–5.38).
- **The visor on the cursor did not register.** Judges read the visor cursor as a plain haloed cursor; the visor is too small to see.
- **Still unanswered:** no mark carries "the owner's control".

Honest summary: on score, the **haloed narrow cloud** and the **cloud with the visor** are tied first. On meaning, the **halo bot with a beam** says the most ("an AI that controls blocks") but looks weaker.
