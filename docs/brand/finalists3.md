# Round six: almighty from above, against no. 2

Artem's brief: more variants in a new direction (clouds, a god-like presence, strings), keeping finalist no. 2 (the block head) as the mark to beat. Sheet: [`finalists3.png`](finalists3.png). Row 0 is no. 2 for comparison. Each row shows the icon large, as a store list card at real size, and at 64 and 32 px. Single files: `finalists3/`. All 13 sketches: [`clouds32.png`](clouds32.png). Source: `scripts/brand/clouds32.py`, `round6.py`, `finalists3.py`.

## Result

**Visor cloud on strings** (row 1) beats no. 2. A cloud wears no. 2's terminal visor and eyes and works a floating island on strings.

- **Head to head:** three fresh comparison judges saw no. 2, the visor cloud and the plain cloud side by side. All three picked the visor cloud for the meaning ("a dark, watchful machine hovers above a floating island and holds it up with beams. That reads as an AI controlling my world from above") and for the click. On craft they rated it professional once and "decent, leaning professional" twice. No. 2 got amateur twice and decent once ("just a robot face… says nothing about the world").
- **Alone at a glance**, it is the closest icon-only read of any round: "a robot or machine face holding a floating grass block island … a world or chunk tool, maybe a world-management … mod".
- **As a list card**, both judges understood the mod and said they would probably click.
- **Harsh look check:** decent, and judged hand-made, not AI output. The notes: the cloud is cropped at the top, the island is small, the beams are one pixel.

## Process

1. **13 concepts at 32 px**, all with square clouds drawn in the vanilla manner: the terminal face in the clouds, clouds working a player or a block on strings, a hand from the clouds, eyes in the sky, a beam from a cloud, a command block in the clouds, a crowned face, a cloud that is a face, and a cursor from a cloud.
2. **Feed judge** on each: one icon-alone glance (32 and 64 px) and one list card (Almighty among three ordinary mods). Every judge was a fresh `claude -p` session in an empty directory that saw only that image.
3. **Cut to four**, by the glance reads: the cloud face ("robot or ghost face"), eyes in the sky ("robot face"), the crowned face ("crowned king"), and a cloud working an island on strings ("island hanging from a cloud… clear, I'd click"). Hands, cursors and command blocks in clouds read as a rocket, a lamp and a chest.
4. **Refined at 64 px** (hue-shifted ramps, an outline only on the outer silhouette, softened steps, a two-tone sky tile) and retested: two glance reads, two card reads and a harsh look check each.
5. **Comparison against no. 2**, then one merge pass: no. 2's visor on the cloud that works the island. Then a final retest and comparison.
