Fixes after playing on the server on 04.10.

- A bot pilot no longer loses its aircraft. When a vehicle driven by a bot (Immersive Aircraft planes) flew into an area where the world did not tick entities, the bot fell out of its seat and the aircraft froze at the edge of the loaded chunks. The bot now stays seated, and its chunks follow the vehicle.

## Installation

The "Airstrike Pack" modpack installs Almighty only on the server. Without the modpack: replace `almighty-0.2.0.jar` in the server's `mods` folder with `almighty-0.2.1.jar`. Players install nothing.
