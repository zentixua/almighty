The game master drives vehicles more reliably: fixes from playing on 04.10.

- Bots now have a "dead man's switch". The `hold` step accepts a `ticks` duration: the key releases itself unless renewed. If a rule controlling a bot turns off (because of its budget, errors or a flood of feed entries), the bot stops: programs are removed, keys are released, and the feed receives a `bot_stopped` event. Previously, when an Immersive Aircraft plane's autopilot rule turned off, its held control stayed pressed, and the aircraft went into the sea.
- A script can replace a bot's programs by calling `bot.act(steps, true)`, like `replace` in the `bot.act` method.
- Sable (Create Aeronautics) ship autopilots can now see the ships themselves: method `ships`, and `gm.ship(name)` and `gm.ships()` in scripts. They expose the name, position, rotation, velocity in blocks per second, angular velocity, bounds in the world and on the plot, and the ship's block entities by type (engines, burners). Access goes through Sable's public library (sable-companion), without mixins. Without Sable, the method reports that ships are unavailable.

## Installation

The "Airstrike Pack" modpack installs Almighty only on the server. Without the modpack: replace `almighty-0.2.2.jar` in the server's `mods` folder with `almighty-0.3.0.jar`. Players install nothing.
